Eat like a local, while traveling abroad

Discover foreign language menus the way a local would describe them, and leave your translation apps at home.

The Problem

Apps translate menu text, but they don't help you order with confidence.

Millions of travelers eat at restaurants abroad with menus they cannot read. Even with translation tools, the gap between understanding words and confidently placing an order is enormous.

Lost in translation

Specialty and regional dishes often yield strange or meaningless translations, turning the most exciting items on the menu into the most confusing ones.

No real context

Without context for ingredients, taste, and dietary considerations, ordering becomes guesswork rather than a confident decision.

No way to actually order

There is no existing app that guides a traveler from an unreadable foreign menu to a confident, placed order.

How It Works

Menular guides travelers from an unreadable foreign menu to placing an order with confidence.
