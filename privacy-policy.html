Information we collect

Account information. When you sign in, we receive your name and email address from Apple (Sign in with Apple) or Google (Google Sign In). We do not offer email and password sign-in, and we never receive or store your Apple or Google password. For users who sign in with Apple, we store an Apple authorization token on our servers. We use this token only to revoke your access if you delete your account.

Profile and preferences. Your display name, your native language, your preferred currency, and your location-detection setting.

Content you create. The Ask Staff Questions you write (for example, dietary or allergen questions), the menus you favorite, and the words, phrases, and dishes you bookmark for learning.

Menu scan data. When you scan a menu, we keep the extracted, structured text of the menu (such as the restaurant name and the dish and beverage information). If your location-detection setting is on, we also store the general location of the scan (city, region, and country).

Usage history. A record of which menus you have scanned and when, so you can return to them and filter them.

Purchase information. When you buy a scan pass, a scan credit pack, or a Language Learner add-on, we store the Apple StoreKit transaction identifier, the price paid, and the details of what you purchased. Apple, not Menular, processes your payment, so Menular never receives or stores your payment card number or any other payment information.

Device location. We only use your device location if you turn on location detection. When it is on, we use your approximate location for two purposes: to detect the country and currency of a scanned menu so we can convert prices into your home currency, and to let you filter your scan history by the location where each menu was scanned. If location detection is off, we do not access your location.

What we do not collect or store

Menu photos. The photo you take of a menu is used only to extract text (optical character recognition) and is then discarded. We do not store menu images. Only the extracted text that reads as menu content is kept. Background content that does not read as a menu is not retained.

Payment information. As noted above, Menular does not collect or store any payment card or billing information. All payments are handled by Apple.

Third-party analytics and advertising trackers. Menular does not use any third-party analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or cross-app tracking software. We do keep basic internal operational logs about each scan (such as the size of the menu and its processing cost) to run and improve the Service. These logs are not linked to your identity and contain no user account information.

Pre-account usage

You can use a limited number of free scans before you sign in. During this time, your scans and the resulting menu data are stored only on your device, not on our servers, and are not tied to any account. This on-device free-scan count resets if you reinstall the app.

When you sign in for the first time, Menular transfers the menus you scanned on your device into your new account, so you do not lose the work you did before signing in. After you sign in, your data is stored and handled as described in the rest of this policy.

How we use your information

We use the information above to:

  • Provide the core service: scanning menus, translating and enriching dishes, generating a staff-facing order, and producing pronunciation audio.
  • Save your menus, favorites, bookmarks, staff questions, and history so you can return to them across your trips, and let you filter your history by location.
  • Detect the correct country and currency for a menu and convert prices into your home currency (only when location detection is on).
  • Process, restore, and reconcile your purchases and track your scan entitlements.
  • Transfer the menus you scanned before signing in into your account when you first sign in.
  • Maintain the security of the app, prevent abuse, and comply with our legal obligations.

How your information is shared

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising or cross-context behavioral advertising. Menular contains no ads, and we do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to enforce our Terms, or to protect the rights, safety, and property of Menular, our users, or others.

Payments

Payments are handled by Apple through StoreKit, and purchases are managed through RevenueCat. We store a record of each purchase (the transaction identifier, the price, and what was bought) so we can deliver, restore, and reconcile your purchases. Menular does not collect or store any payment card number or billing information.

International users and data transfers

Menular is operated from the United States, and the service providers above process data in the United States and other countries. If you use Menular from outside the United States, including from the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards, such as Standard Contractual Clauses, for these transfers.

How long we keep your information

We keep your account information and content for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, we permanently remove your profile, menus, favorites, bookmarks, staff questions, and scan history (see Account deletion below).

We retain a de-identified record of your purchases (with no personal link back to you) after account deletion, because we need it to meet tax, accounting, and payment-reconciliation obligations. We keep these de-identified records for up to 7 years, consistent with financial record-keeping requirements.

Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have some or all of the following rights over your personal information:

  • Access: request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction: ask us to correct inaccurate information.
  • Deletion: delete your account and associated data at any time from within the app (Settings), or by contacting us.
  • Portability: request your information in a portable format.
  • Objection and restriction: object to or ask us to restrict certain processing.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom (GDPR), these rights apply to you, and you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

If you are a California resident (CCPA/CPRA), you have the right to know what personal information we collect and how we use it, the right to delete it, the right to correct it, and the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. As stated above, we do not sell or share personal information. We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of these rights.

To exercise any of these rights, use the in-app deletion option or email us at support@cosible.com. We will respond within the time required by applicable law.

Account deletion

You can delete your account at any time from the Settings screen in the app. When you delete your account, we permanently and immediately remove your profile, your saved and favorited menus, your bookmarks, your staff questions, and your scan history. This is a permanent deletion, not a temporary deactivation, and it cannot be undone.

If you signed in with Apple, we also revoke your Apple authorization token at the time of deletion.

As noted above, we retain a de-identified record of your past purchases for tax and reconciliation purposes. This record is no longer linked to your identity after deletion.

Deleting your account affects your entitlements. Any unused scan credits, an active scan pass, or remaining bookmark capacity from a Language Learner add-on are permanently forfeited on deletion and cannot be restored. See the Terms of Service for details.

Children

Menular is not directed to children. You must be at least 16 years old to use Menular. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child under 16 has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including authentication through Supabase and secure handling of your data by the providers listed above. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top and, where appropriate, provide additional notice in the app. Your continued use of Menular after an update means you accept the revised policy.
