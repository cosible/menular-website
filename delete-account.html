Delete your Menular account

Deleting your account permanently removes your menus, bookmarks, and other saved data from Menular. Account deletion cannot be undone.

Recommended: Use the Menular App Delete Account Feature

Menular lets you delete your account directly in the app. This feature works for every account, whether you signed in or used Menular as a guest, and deletes your account and data immediately.

  1. Open Menular and tap the settings icon in the upper right corner of the Home screen.
  2. Tap Delete Account and follow the prompts to delete.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile and preferences
  • Your scanned and favorite menus
  • Your bookmarks and Ask Staff questions
  • Your scan history
  • Any unused scans, active scan pass, or remaining bookmark capacity. These cannot be restored.

What we keep

  • A record of past purchases, no longer linked to your account, kept for up to 7 years for tax and accounting.
  • On Android, an anonymous free-scan counter. This counter only ensures each phone receives its five free scans once and is not linked to your account, name, or email.

See our Privacy Policy for details.

Request Account Deletion via Email

If you cannot use the app, you may request account deletion by email. Email requests are only possible if you signed in to Menular with Apple or Google and still have access to the email address used to sign in. Guest accounts have no associated email address, so we cannot identify them.

When you send your request:

  • Send the request from the email address you used to sign in. If that is not possible, include the sign-in email address in your message.
  • If you signed in with Apple's Hide My Email, include your private address ending in @privaterelay.appleid.com. On your iPhone, this address is listed in Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Hide My Email.

What to expect after you send your request: we will reply to confirm that the request came from you, then delete your account within 30 days and notify you by email once the deletion is complete. If anything prevents us from deleting your account, we will notify you by email.
