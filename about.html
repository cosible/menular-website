Eat like a local, while traveling abroad

Discover foreign language menus the way a local would describe them, and leave your translation apps at home

The Problem

Apps translate menu text, but they don't help you order with confidence

Millions of travelers eat at restaurants abroad with menus they cannot read. Translation apps convert the words but don't help you discover what's really on the menu and order with confidence.

Lost in translation

Specialty and regional dishes often yield strange or meaningless translations, turning the most exciting items on the menu into the most confusing ones.

No real context

Without context for ingredients, taste, and what makes a dish worth trying, your order is guesswork.

Beyond translation

Most apps stop at translation. Menular takes you all the way to a placed order.

How It Works

From an unreadable menu to a confident order, in these steps...

1

Scan

Point your camera at any restaurant or bar menu and let Menular do the reading

2

Discover

Every dish and drink explained from ingredients and taste to preparation and what makes it worth ordering

3

Build

Add what looks good to your order the way you'd shop online. No guessing required

4

Place Order

Show your order to restaurant staff in their language, and order without saying a word

The Menular Story

Menular comes from menu + vernacular, the local language of food and culture. The best way to discover what's on a menu is to hear it the way a local would describe it. That idea lies at the heart of Menular.

I'm Claudio, and my vision for Menular began during an extended trip to China where I struggled to order confidently in local restaurants. I speak Mandarin well enough to hold a conversation, but still couldn't experience the true depth of local cuisine in the places I visited. Menular exists to change that, for travelers everywhere!
